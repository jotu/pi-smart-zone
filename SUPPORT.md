# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** open a [feature request or question issue](https://github.com/jotu/pi-smart-zone/issues/new/choose).
- **Bugs:** use the bug report form and include your Pi and Node.js versions.
- **Security:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.

This is a small, volunteer-maintained project; responses are best effort.
